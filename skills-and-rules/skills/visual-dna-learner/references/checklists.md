# What to look for

Checklists for step 3–5 of the skill. Use what's relevant; don't fill in every line for every video.

## Story beats → visual beats
Beats: hook, curiosity gap, premise, problem, explanation, example, contrast, escalation, reveal, payoff, CTA, emotional shift.
For each visual change, note what triggered it: a new idea, an important word, an emotion change, an example, a punchline, a number, a logic turn, an audio cue, or just a visual opportunity. The goal is the mapping from narrative beats to layouts and assets.

## Shots
A-roll, B-roll, object, product, environment, screen recording, screenshot, UI, photo, archive, illustration, infographic, diagram, map, 2.5D, 3D, type-only, graphic-only, visual metaphor, mixed media.
For each: when it appears, what narration triggers it, typical length, composition, information vs mood, literal vs metaphor, enters before/on/after the spoken point.

## Composition
Subject placement and head height, visual centre, negative space, text-safe zones, object scale, foreground/background separation, depth and layer count, symmetry, crop strategy, background complexity.

## Camera
Static, micro/slow/fast push, pull-back, lateral/vertical move, punch-in/out, reframe, handheld, parallax, 2.5D/3D camera, whip, simulated movement on stills. For each recurring move: purpose, direction, intensity, duration (from a strip), relation to narration, linear/eased/spring.

## Assets
What the creator shows for: a person, data, a process, an abstract idea, a topic change, an emotional peak. Literal / contextual / metaphorical / decorative. Source: original footage, stock, screen recording, screenshot, archive, photo, illustration, 3D, AI, physical objects. Treatment: cutout, halftone, B/W with colour accent, paper edges, drop shadows.

## Graphics
Cards, labels, arrows, circles, highlights, diagrams, charts, counters, timelines, maps, annotations, icons, masks, textures, stickers. For each: function, trigger, entrance, movement, exit, relation to footage and to type.

## Typography
Is text used, when, and when is it deliberately absent? Caption role vs title role vs label role. Word count, placement, hierarchy, font character, weight, colour, box/stroke/shadow, reveal method (type-on, slide, pop), timing to voice. Decide whether type is the storytelling mechanism or reinforcement.

## Pacing
Shot/insert lengths (min/median/max), visual changes per minute, A-roll hold lengths and what's said during them, density during lists, holds before a payoff. No universal "change every X seconds" rule — only conditional patterns with evidence.

## Audio (needs listening — say "not verified" if you can't)
Music presence and changes, SFX density (whoosh, impact, click, paper), silence, J/L-cuts, audio-led cuts. Which sounds cause visual changes and which just accompany them.

## Transitions
Hard cut, match cut, graphic/motion match, push, slide, wipe, mask, paper tear, zoom-through, occlusion, dissolve. Frequency, context, direction, duration in frames, and why it fits.
